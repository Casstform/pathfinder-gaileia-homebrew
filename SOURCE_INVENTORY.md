## Deliberately excluded

- `Pathfinder 2e Homebrew Links` is a reference-link list, not original homebrew content.
- `Baked Spider Legs`, `Necklace of Knives`, and `Firecracker Fulu` were verified as published Paizo rules/reference cards, not original homebrew, so they were not republished as homebrew entries.
- The older Forge JSON was superseded by the newer Forge record. The broken revolver JSON was merged with the existing damaged-revolver card rather than duplicated.
- `Homebrew Item Ideas` and the unfinished apparition notes are brainstorm/reference material rather than table-complete entries. They remain source leads for a later, explicitly reviewed draft batch.
- `saddlebrown_grey.json` is presentation data, not game content. PSDs and standalone art assets were likewise excluded.
- The source PDFs and screenshots are not published; the web transcriptions are the canonical site copy.
- Official spell descriptions are not reproduced in full unless the wording is part of the user-selected final homebrew source. The Echoes spell list links outward whenever a current Archives of Nethys entry was available.

## Future import rule

List the known Drive folder first, then fetch only the exact new file. Never make a Drive-wide search a prerequisite for publishing or updating the rest of the catalogue. A missing or unreadable source becomes one explicit inventory gap while every complete entry continues through validation and release.
